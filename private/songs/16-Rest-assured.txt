Rest assured
Theme: Confirmation

<i>Selections from the Writings of ‘Abdu’l‑Bahá</i>, #216
https://www.bahai.org/r/858538367


Rest assured (x3)
that the breathings (x3)
of the Holy (x3)
Spirit
will loosen (x3)
thy tongue.
Speak, therefore; (x2)
speak out
with great
(with great) courage
at every meeting.
I am with you
heart and soul
at every meeting;
heart and soul
at every meeting; (x2)
be sure of this. (x4)
