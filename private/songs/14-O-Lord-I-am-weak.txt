O Lord! I am weak
Theme: Confirmation

‘Abdu’l‑Bahá, <i>Bahá’í Prayers</i>
https://www.bahai.org/r/091008417


CallAndResponse: true
O Lord!
I am weak,
strengthen me
* with Thy power and potency.
My tongue falters,
suffer me to utter
* Thy commemoration and praise.
I am lowly,
honor me
through admitting
me into Thy kingdom.
