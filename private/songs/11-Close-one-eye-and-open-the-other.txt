Close one eye and open the other
Theme: Material World

Bahá’u’lláh, <i>The Hidden Words</i>, From the Persian, #12
https://www.bahai.org/r/775762706


O man of two visions! (x2)
Close one eye,
and open the other. (x2)
Close one to the world
and all that is therein,
and open the other
to the hallowed beauty
of the Beloved. (x2)
