O let the flame
Theme: Love

<i>Gleanings from the Writings of Bahá'u'lláh</i>, CLIII
https://www.bahai.org/r/413529355


CallAndResponse: true
O let the flame
of the love of God
burn brightly within
your radiant hearts.
(x2)

Feed it with the oil of
Divine guidance, and
protect it within the shelter
of your constancy.
(x2)
