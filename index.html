About

This website was created by individual initiative to share lyrics to Bahá'í songs sung in devotional spaces, with an emphasis on songs created in the San Diego Cluster Youth Camps.

To view similar content in Google-Slide form, see bit.ly/BahaiSongs.

Request a Song

To request to add a song, please email sdbahaisongs@gmail.com with the song title, link, and lyrics.

Optionally, you can include a video of the song and/or its guitar chords.
